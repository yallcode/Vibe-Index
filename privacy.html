Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This page explains what data vibemodded index collects when you use the site, and why. If anything here is unclear, the Discord link at the bottom is the fastest way to reach us.

What we collect when you log in

Logging in uses GitHub through Firebase Authentication. When you do, we receive and store your GitHub username, your display name, and your avatar image. This is what lets the site show your name and picture next to mods you submit, and is how we know a mod really belongs to the account that is submitting it.

What we collect when you submit a mod

When you submit a mod, we read your repository's mod.json file, and its about.md file if one exists, directly from GitHub. This gives us the mod's name, description, version, tags, and supported platforms. We also store the repository link and the download link for your mod's latest release. None of this is private information, it is all already public on your GitHub repository, we are just copying the relevant parts into our own database so the site can list it.

Download counts

Each mod has a download counter that goes up when someone clicks its Download button. This count is not tied to who clicked it, we do not know or store who downloaded what.

What we do not collect

We do not use analytics or tracking cookies on this site. We do not collect your email address. We do not sell or share your data with advertisers, and we have none to sell in the first place.

Where your data lives

Everything is stored on Firebase, which is Google Cloud infrastructure. Google's own privacy policy covers how that underlying infrastructure handles data. Nothing here changes or overrides that, it just adds what we ourselves choose to store on top of it.

Your rights

You can ask us what data we have about you, or ask us to delete your account and everything tied to it, including any mods you submitted. To do either, reach out on our Discord, linked below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change with it.
